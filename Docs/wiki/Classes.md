## Sorcery

### Active skills

#### Fireball

Projectile is not spawned.

#### Freezing Arrow

Projectile is not spawned.

#### Insulating Lens

Buff is not applied.

#### Arc Lightning

Nothing happens.

#### Freezing Earth

Spell is cast. It does not do anything (no debuff, no damage).

#### Searing Rain

Channels, animation loop. No projectiles.

#### Frigid Tracks

No buff applied.

#### Magic Circle

Nothing happens.

#### Chain Lightning

Does damage, but bounces weirdly. Shows a cast bar every few seconds.

#### Flame Barrier

Nothing happens. Cast bar is glitched after casting.

#### Meteor

Projectiles appear. Damage is dealt on the caster instead of at the target.
Same issue applies to debuffs.
